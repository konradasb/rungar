# Maintainers

This document lists the maintainers of Rungar.

Maintainers are responsible for reviewing and merging pull requests, triaging issues, cutting releases, and guiding the technical direction of the project.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

- Respond to issues and pull requests in a timely manner
- Review and merge contributions that align with the project's goals
- Ensure the project's quality, security, and roadmap
- Cut releases following the release process
- Uphold and enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Becoming a Maintainer

Consistent contributors who have demonstrated technical depth and alignment with project goals may be nominated as maintainers. The process is:

1. A current maintainer nominates the contributor by opening a GitHub issue
2. Existing maintainers discuss and reach consensus (lazy consensus — no objections within 7 days means approval)
3. The new maintainer is added to this file and granted repository access

## Stepping Down

Maintainers who wish to step down should open a PR removing themselves from this file. Inactive maintainers (no contributions or reviews for 6+ months) may be moved to emeritus status after being contacted.
